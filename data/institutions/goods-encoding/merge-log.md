Both encoders recorded nothing as not_expressible. Both kept `has_own_income` (declared only in coverture-floor) in `provided_for`, accepting the constant-false warning elsewhere; both wrote `child_with_family` as one disjunct per entry because no fluent is shared across cps, kibbutz and ectogenesis.

**What the agreement does and does not show.** Pass item "at least 3 goods written independently by a second encoder" is met in form: 5 goods, both encoders, audited. But the two encoders are two instances of the same model given the same bundle, and both copied the one-step grace from the `care_duty_on_women` style example into `cared_for`, `known_carer` and `child_with_family`. Four of five equivalent is therefore weak evidence that goods can be encoded without the encoder's own picture of the good (plans/priority.md "Open questions"): it measures one model's consistency, not agreement between people. Every report says: second encoder: agent, not someone living under the institution (ADR 0012). A stronger test, not run: a human second encoder, a different model family, or a bundle with no style example carrying the grace.

**Disclosure (added 2026-11-06 after docs/reviews/2026-11-06-slice2-faithfulness-review.md finding 6).** The encoder prompts carried instructions that are not in the hashed bundle. Both prompts were identical except for the encoder number, and both said, besides the access rules and the output format: "write ONE state formula ... usable across the entries listed for it (and the comparison entries); if one formula cannot serve every listed entry because the vocabularies differ, prefer a formula that uses only fluents shared across them; if an entry's vocabulary cannot carry the good at all, record it under not_expressible"; "Never invent fluents"; and "treat an undeclared-fluent warning as a sign the formula does not fit there (either rewrite or record not_expressible)". The coordinator wrote these; the protocol (plans/priority-slice2.md section 2) did not specify them. The shared-fluent preference is a plausible cause of both encoders guarding every good on `is_subject` and `provides_care`, which the review links to the police-crisis and kibbutz misses. The statement above that the bundle was the encoders' only input is therefore incomplete: the bundle plus this prompt text was. The prompts are recorded in the coordinator's session transcript; the text quoted here is from them.

**Headline for the experiment** (as restated after the revision-3 faithfulness review, finding 6; the first wording, "the earlier agreement came largely from the gloss fixing the vocabulary", claimed a cause the experiment does not isolate). With glosses written blind (from theory.md and intent.md only), the gloss-to-formula step is underdetermined for this model on five of six goods: two Claude encoders on one hashed bundle agree (z3) on 1 of 6. The ids do not fix the goods: no blind formula is equivalent to the dataset's, because the blind author wrote different goods under the same ids (care without control, provision as own means, chosen care with own income); that shows the dataset formulas encode the non-blind author's goods, not that they misencode their own glosses. The theory documents alone did not produce three of the six goods (mutual household care, chosen care, child with family: the selection of goods is the non-blind author's choice). The drop from revision 1's 4 of 5 is consistent with the gloss having fixed the fluents (revision-2 faithfulness finding 9) but its cause is not isolated: the gloss content, the prompt's cross-entry instruction, the vocabulary (`care_obliged`, `has_own_income`) and the conventions also changed, and all six goods were encoded together. "Blind" covers the gloss text only: ids, `entries` lists, vocabularies and conventions were shared non-blind inputs. One gloss author (gloss and encoder variance confounded) and one model throughout: no bound on cross-model or human agreement. Proposed follow-up, **not run**: two fresh encoders on bundle3x with the revision-1 glosses substituted; about 4 of 5 agreement would point to the gloss as the cause. Notable divergences in the blind encodings: `cared_for` adds "the carer holds no power to sanction the subject"; `child_with_family` is written for cps only (kibbutz and ectogenesis not expressible); `provided_for` requires means of her own rather than maintenance; `chosen_mutual_care` adds her own income. Experiment formulas do not enter the dataset (R3.6 fail condition); the council recommends no replacement (results, "Revision 3 fix pass"; the author decides). Second encoder: agent, not someone living under the institution (ADR 0012).
