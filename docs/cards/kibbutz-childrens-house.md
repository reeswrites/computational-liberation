# kibbutz-childrens-house

**Kibbutz children's house (communal sleeping, metapelet care rota)**

> 'Children's lives had three focal points: the children's house, parents' house, and the whole kibbutz.' 'They lived in the children's house, where they had communal sleeping arrangements' and 'visited their parents for 2-3 hours a day.' 'The nanny position was always a woman's role. Teacher positions were both for women and men.' (Wikipedia, 'Kibbutz communal child rearing and collective educat...

## Roles

- **committee**: the work / education committee that staffs the children's house (the kibbutz general assembly's organ)
- **metapelet**: the metapelet (nanny / caregiver) assigned to the children's house; a woman
- **male_member**: a male kibbutz member; a parent who visits, never a children's-house carer
- **child**: a child of the kibbutz; sleeps in the children's house
