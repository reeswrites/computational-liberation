## Roles

- **family**: the household deciding for a daughter (Mackie's 'parents' who bind, or pledge not to); the institutional sort, not a demographic (ADR 0011)
- **daughter**: the subject: the girl whose feet are bound or left unbound; bound in childhood, so she decides nothing
- **suitor**: the family of a prospective groom: the marriage-market counterparty whose expectations hold the convention

## Notes

Layer-2 gate (a) institution. Mackie's convention: a family binds because every other family binds and suitors expect it (R3 sanctioned norm, R5 the market's refusal, R6/R7 the marriage condition); the campaign's pledge associations (R4) create a marriage pool in which an unbound daughter is marriageable, so the sanction R5 lapses for pledged families and leave_unbound (R2) becomes costless once enough neighbours pledge. Deviations from the task sketch: no matchmaker/community role (no source gives one a rule); a `suitor` role instead, because the sanction needs an actor and Mackie's sanction is the other side of the market; the pledge's son half is a second pledge action R10 accept_match_pledge (suitor) read by R5/R6 (until 2026-11-04 it was folded into the family's pledge; encoding review finding 2); marriageable is derived (ADR 0013) from R7 rather than asserted; R3 is unconditional and R5 carries the (not pledged) condition, so that the pledge changes nothing at layer 1 (the preregistration the gate pair needs).
